The file discovery/catalogue.db is a catalogue of web feeds built by Morning Issue's own tool
(tools/discovery/build_catalogue.py) from three openly licensed lists of feed addresses. Each
feed was fetched once when the catalogue was built; the titles, descriptions and headlines it
holds are those the feed's publisher put in the feed, and belong to that publisher.
